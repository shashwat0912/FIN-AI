# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing their personal finances who need to review activity, make decisions, and act with confidence in a focused workspace. They may return frequently, often with limited attention, and need financial data to remain legible across desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

FinanceAI brings transactions, budgets, goals, and financial guidance into one coherent workspace. Success means users can understand their position, record financial activity, and decide what to do next without visual noise or unnecessary ceremony.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and mature. The product should feel like an intelligent private financial notebook: editorial and premium, but never flashy.

## Anti-references

Generic dark fintech dashboards, card-heavy SaaS templates, glowing AI interfaces, chat bolted onto an unrelated product, glassmorphism, gradients, purple accents, oversized radii, and decorative financial metrics.

## Design Principles

- Make financial data the dominant visual material.
- Use typography, spacing, and rules before adding surfaces.
- Reserve emphasis for the decision or action that matters now.
- Keep AI guidance native to the financial workflow and structured for action.
- Prefer quiet familiarity, exact language, and accessible state feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, persistent form labels, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, color-independent state cues, responsive layouts, and localization-safe content lengths.
